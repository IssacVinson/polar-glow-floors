Carpet cleaning made for Alaska floors

Life here is hard on carpet. Snow and slush come in on boots for months, gravel and road grit get tracked in from the Glenn Highway, and breakup season brings mud to every entryway. Dirt that works into carpet fibers wears them down over time. Regular carpet cleaning keeps high-traffic rooms looking better and feeling fresh.

  • Carpeted rooms: living rooms, bedrooms, offices, dining rooms, hallways and bathrooms
  • Stairs: one flat price for the staircase
  • Commercial carpet: offices, shops and businesses, call for a quote

Frequently asked questions

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

A living room is $140. Bedrooms, offices, dining rooms and stairs are $80 each. Bathrooms, hallways and rugs are $40 each.

Which areas do you serve?

We're based in Eagle River and clean carpets in Eagle River, Chugiak, Anchorage, Wasilla, Palmer and Girdwood, including JBER housing when the resident arranges base access. Girdwood is a longer drive, so call to confirm scheduling.

Do you clean couches and rugs too?

Yes. A couch with 2 or more seats is $120, a single-seat couch is $70, and rugs are $40 each.

Can you get pet odor out of carpet?

Often we can make a real difference, but we won't promise every odor will be fully removed, especially if it has soaked into the padding. We'll tell you honestly what to expect.

What is the free stain demo?

We'll clean one stain for free. In exchange, we ask you to leave an honest review of the result.

How do I book?

We work by appointment only. Call or text (907) 518-4614, tell us which rooms and items you want cleaned, and we'll confirm your price and pick a time together.
