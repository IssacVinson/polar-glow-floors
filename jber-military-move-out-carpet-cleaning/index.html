Move-Out Carpet Cleaning for JBER and Military Families

Check your housing office requirements first

Every housing office, landlord, and property manager sets its own move-out rules. Some require professional carpet cleaning. Some ask for a receipt. Some have their own specific requirements. We can't promise that our cleaning will meet any particular inspection standard, so please check with your housing office or landlord before you book. Once you know what they need, call us and we'll talk through whether our service fits.

Base access

We're a local civilian business. If your home is on JBER, you'll need to arrange base access for us through your normal visitor process before your appointment. If your home is off base, just give us the address.

What about pet stains and odors?

Pets are part of a lot of military families, and we understand the worry about a pet-related charge at move-out. We'll work hard on pet stains and odors, but we'll be honest with you: some odors soak deep into the padding or subfloor, and carpet cleaning can't always remove them completely. If you have a problem area, tell us when you book so we can set realistic expectations together.

Couches and rugs too

If you're keeping your furniture and shipping it to your next duty station, you can have your couch or area rugs cleaned in the same visit. They count toward the 10% multi-item discount.

Try us with a free stain demo

Not sure yet? We offer a free single-stain demo in exchange for an honest review. It's an easy way to see our work before you book a full move-out cleaning.

Booking

We work by appointment only. Move-out timelines can be tight, so reach out as soon as you have your dates and we'll find an appointment that works for your schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Will your cleaning pass my JBER housing inspection?

We can't guarantee that our cleaning will meet any specific inspection standard. Requirements vary by housing office and landlord, so please confirm what yours requires before booking.

Can you get onto base?

You'll need to arrange base access for us through your usual visitor process. We're glad to coordinate the details with you when you book.

Do you charge for a hallway or bathroom?

Yes, $40 each. Rugs are also $40 each.

Can you remove pet odors?

We'll do our best, but we won't promise complete removal. Odors that have reached the padding or subfloor may not come out fully with carpet cleaning.

Do you serve off-base rentals?

Yes. We clean homes in Eagle River, Chugiak, Anchorage, and nearby areas, both on and off base.
