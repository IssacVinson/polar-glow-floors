Frequently asked questions

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Anchorage with Polar Glow Floors?

A living room is $140. Bedrooms, offices, and dining rooms are $80 each. Stairs are $80, and bathrooms, hallways, and rugs are $40 each.

Do you clean couches and rugs too?

Yes. A couch with 2 or more seats is $120, a single-seat couch is $70, and rugs are $40 each. They can go on the same appointment as your carpet cleaning.
