An honest answer about pet odor

Here's what many carpet cleaners won't tell you: not every pet odor can be fully removed. Fresh accidents on the carpet surface often respond well to cleaning. When urine has soaked through to the padding or the subfloor, though, cleaning the carpet may reduce the smell without getting rid of it. Some stains also change the carpet's color for good.

We'd rather set honest expectations up front than make a promise we can't keep. When you call, tell us how old the accidents are, how many spots there are, and what type of pet made them. That helps us talk through what's realistic for your home before we book anything.

Frequently asked questions

Can you completely remove pet urine odor from my carpet?

Sometimes, but not always. Surface-level accidents often clean up well. If urine has reached the padding or subfloor, carpet cleaning may reduce the odor without fully removing it. We'll give you an honest assessment when you call.

Will old pet stains come out?

It depends on the stain. Some old stains lift; others have permanently changed the carpet fibers' color. The free single-stain demo is a good way to find out before you commit.

Do you clean pet hair and accidents off couches?

Yes. We clean upholstery: $120 for a couch with 2 or more seats and $70 for a single-seat couch.
